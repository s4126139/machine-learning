# Week 10 Tutorial Questions — Clustering

Source: Canvas module item [8460563](https://rmit.instructure.com/courses/171525/modules/items/8460563)

Captured: 2026-09-24

## Objectives

- Revise K-Means.
- Revise hierarchical clustering.

## Questions

1. What is the goal of clustering in unsupervised learning? Give one real-world
   example where clustering could be useful.
2. In K-Means, what does the algorithm try to minimise during training?
3. Why does the choice of the number of clusters (`k`) matter in K-Means? What
   happens if `k` is too small or too large?
4. What is the difference between agglomerative and divisive hierarchical clustering?
   Which one is more commonly used?
5. What is a dendrogram, and how can it help you decide the number of clusters in
   hierarchical clustering?
