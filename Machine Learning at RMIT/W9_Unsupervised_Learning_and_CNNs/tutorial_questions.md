# Week 9 Tutorial Questions — CNN

Source: Canvas module item [8460548](https://rmit.instructure.com/courses/171525/modules/items/8460548)

Captured: 2026-09-24

## Objectives

- Strengthen conceptual understanding of CNN components.
- Build computational skills such as output-size and parameter-count calculations.
- Prepare for real-world CNN design and optimisation.
- Develop critical thinking about architecture choices.
- Build confidence before practical coding work.

## Questions

1. What is the main purpose of convolutional layers in CNNs compared with fully
   connected layers? Why are CNNs better for images than MLPs?
2. Explain the concepts of a filter (kernel), stride, and padding in a convolutional
   operation.
3. What are “valid padding” and “same padding”?
4. What is the role of pooling layers, and why is max pooling often used?
5. Given a `64 × 64 × 3` input image and a convolutional layer with 32 filters of
   size `3 × 3`, stride 1, and “same padding”, what is the output shape?
6. A convolutional layer has 16 filters, each of size `5 × 5`, and the input has
   3 channels. How many trainable parameters (weights and biases, with one bias per
   filter) are there?
