# Week 8 Tutorial Questions — Perceptrons and MLPs

Source: Canvas [W8 Tutorial Questions](https://rmit.instructure.com/courses/171525/modules/items/8460534)

Captured: 2026-09-24

The seven questions ask students to:

1. Compute outputs for a three-input Perceptron with weights `w₁ = −1`, `w₂ = 0.5`, and `w₃ = 0.2` on two supplied data points.
2. Compare a Perceptron with logistic regression.
3. Propose a way to train Perceptron weights from examples.
4. Identify what is missing from a Perceptron or how its structure could be improved for training.
5. Combine multiple Perceptrons to build binary and n-ary classifiers.
6. Explain how dropout can improve MLP training and performance.
7. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of manually configuring connections between early MLP layers.
