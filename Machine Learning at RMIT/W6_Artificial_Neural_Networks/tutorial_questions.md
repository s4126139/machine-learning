# Week 6 Tutorial Questions — Neural Networks

Source: Canvas module item 8460514

## Objectives

- Revise the Perceptron and Multilayer Perceptron (MLP).
- Practise the backpropagation chain rule.
- Revise how to train an Artificial Neural Network (ANN).

## Questions

1. What is a Perceptron? Describe its components and how it performs binary
   classification.
2. What is the role of the activation function in a neural network? Compare sigmoid,
   ReLU, and tanh.
3. Explain backpropagation. Why is it essential for training MLPs?
4. What practical challenges occur when training neural networks? Consider data
   preprocessing, network design, and overfitting.
5. How can overfitting be prevented in neural networks? Discuss dropout, weight
   regularisation, and early stopping.
